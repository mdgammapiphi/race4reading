About the Race

Race 4 Reading is Maryland Gamma's annual 5K, bringing together the Hopkins community and beyond for a morning of running, walking, and giving back. Every entry supports Pi Beta Phi's national literacy philanthropy.

Our Mission

Read → Lead → Achieve

Pi Beta Phi's philanthropy is built around literacy — because reading opens the door to leading and achieving. Race 4 Reading channels the energy of a chapter 5K into direct support for youth literacy programs, one mile at a time.
